Project 04 / Meeting Notes AI

Turn a recording into usable notes.

A practical n8n pipeline that transcribes meeting audio, extracts decisions and action items, then sends the result where the team already works.

Upload a file or provide a Google Drive link. Whisper handles the transcript; structured AI output goes to Notion, Slack, and optional email.
